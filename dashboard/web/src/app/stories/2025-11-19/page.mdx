# East Africa Flood Risk · 19 November 2025

The **GIK-IceChain** pipeline assessed flood risk for all 238 admin-1 units
across 16 East African countries on this OND (short-rains) date, combining the
51-member ECMWF IFS ensemble, GPM IMERG observations, and the ICPAC CRMA
Bayesian Network, at near-zero cost on open data.

Risk is computed from four evidence streams:

- **Forecast hazard**: ensemble exceedance probability vs adaptive GEV thresholds
- **Observed antecedent**: GPM IMERG daily precipitation
- **Soil memory**: Antecedent Precipitation Index (API), exponential decay
- **Spatial coverage**: fraction of grid cells with an active signal

## Exceedance probability (24 h, 5-year return period)

The exceedance layer shows the fraction of the 51 ensemble members whose
worst-case 24-hour accumulation exceeds the 5-year return-period GEV threshold.
Values above 0.4 (40% of members) map to a **High** forecast-hazard state.
